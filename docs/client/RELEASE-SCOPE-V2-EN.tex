\section*{2. Edge cases and rules to confirm}

These are proposed safeguards, not additional meeting decisions.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Price changes:} confirmed transactions retain original price/\allowbreak{}fee snapshots. Do unpaid reservations lock prices, and for how long?
\item \textbf{Tax:} completeness determines handling, not an optional tax-free mode. Cogito handles missing data; finance confirms validation, rates, deductions and VAT assumptions.
\item \textbf{Last seat:} expiring reservations and atomic allocation. Late payments/\allowbreak{}repeated webhooks must not duplicate seats, credits or charges. Confirm payment after expiry.
\item \textbf{Unpaid installments:} reminders, overdue status and restricted session access. Confirm grace periods, seat release and rejoining; disclose any automatic charges at checkout.
\item \textbf{Cancellation/\allowbreak{}withdrawal:} distinguish Cogito cancellation, student withdrawal, absence and tutor no-show. Proposed refunds use Marks; plan-specific formulas and cash exceptions require agreement.
\item \textbf{Same-day tutor changes:} check conflicts, notify parties and update access/\allowbreak{}schedules. Earlier fees stay with the previous tutor; agree replacement fees before recording. Confirm rescheduling/\allowbreak{}cancellation without a replacement.
\item \textbf{Friend referrals:} welcome 15 after verification; referrer 15 after first completed private/\allowbreak{}masterclass. Cap 75/\allowbreak{}semester applies only to referrers. Credit once; prevent self-referral. Semester dates, expiry and reversal rules remain open.
\item \textbf{KOL:} confirm any daily redemption/\allowbreak{}conversion limits, timezone, deadlines, compensation and settlement reporting. Attribution does not imply automated payouts.
\item \textbf{Content:} inventory records, files, languages, URLs and relationships. Confirm editor roles, preview/\allowbreak{}publish, backup and rollback. Stop Sanity reads after verification. Multi-day events on different dates are not necessarily duplicates.
\item \textbf{Google:} test new/\allowbreak{}existing accounts, consent, callbacks and email status; fix the confirmed cause.
\end{itemize}
