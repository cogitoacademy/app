## 2. Scope boundary

The detailed functional reference is the Release v3 PRD and its acceptance criteria, including competition catalog requirements REQ-COMP-01..07. The [contract scope comparison](CONTRACT-SCOPE-DELTA.md) distinguishes original obligations from additional work. Existing signup/OTP, authentication, wallet/ledger/payment integration, solo/group/short-series booking, notification/Meet foundations, tutor profiles and basic honorarium visibility are not sold again under this appendix. Defects or unfinished acceptance criteria belonging to the original contract remain original-contract work.

The report work reuses previously agreed/delivered report inputs. The finance work extends existing payout support. Website integration reuses existing website structure and class/profile data. This is not a full website redesign, a replacement payment system, or a rewrite of the existing application.

Before production release, close the decision-register items that gate shipped behavior and agree the retained content/migration inventory. Any separate existing website contract must be checked for overlap so the same deliverable is not charged twice. The Rp5,500,000 fee covers the locked scope in this PRD. A material addition or change to an agreed workflow requires written impact and quotation agreement before that extra work starts; it does not automatically change this fee.

### Not included in this fee

- Feature-video/motion production is still in research and development (R&D). The developer will explore feasibility and make reasonable efforts to develop a suitable approach. Final production scope, any additional fee and delivery schedule will be discussed and agreed in writing after R&D findings are available, before production begins. Video production is not included in the Rp5,500,000 development fee and no finished video is committed at this stage. Placement of an agreed, ready-to-use showcase asset is included in the targeted website work.
- Hosting, domains, Google Workspace, payment gateway charges, storage/email services, paid assets and other third-party subscriptions or usage charges.
- Unspecified bulk content creation/translation or manual entry beyond the agreed migration/content inventory. Cogito supplies or approves English wording; Indonesian is optional.
- New features outside the referenced scope, including automated bank payouts, customer cash refunds, parent accounts, WhatsApp notifications, waitlists, private/school cohorts and a report-template editor.

Any invoice tax treatment or withholding required by the parties must be recorded as part of written acceptance; this quotation does not assume the developer's tax status or add an unspecified tax amount to the price.

## 5. UAT, revisions and defect correction

- This quotation includes **two consolidated UAT feedback rounds** for refinements within the agreed scope. Cogito nominates one approval owner to consolidate feedback and record acceptance against the PRD criteria.
- A defect is behavior that fails an agreed requirement or acceptance criterion. Correcting such a defect is not charged as a new feature and does not become excluded merely because two feedback rounds have occurred.
- New features, changed business rules, additional pages or expanded deliverables are change requests, with impact/price/date agreed before implementation. A material change is not treated as a cosmetic UAT revision.
- Included post-release defect correction period: **30 calendar days from production release of the additions**, covering defects against their agreed scope without an additional development fee. New requirements, ongoing content operations and third-party subscription/usage costs are separate. Original-contract obligations are not redefined by this period.

**Acceptance.** Written agreement to this quotation’s price, scope, payment terms and timeline is required. Record the accepted revision; silence is not acceptance.
